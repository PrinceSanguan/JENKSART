## User-fillable photo slots

`assets/murals/` is created and empty. Each slot renders a designed stand-in
until a file is dropped in. To upgrade the video with Jenks' real work, add:

| Filename | Wanted shot |
|---|---|
| `assets/murals/mural-01.jpg` | The hero finished mural — boldest, most colourful piece |
| `assets/murals/mural-02.jpg` | A second finished mural, different subject/location |
| `assets/murals/mural-03.jpg` | Jenks painting — can with spray in hand, mid-work |
| `assets/murals/mural-04.jpg` | A commercial piece: shopfront, cafe, gym or school wall |

Portrait orientation preferred, ≥1080px on the short edge.
